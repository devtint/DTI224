Hobbies & Interests

Intro

Most of what I do for fun looks a lot like what I do for work, I just don't get graded on it. I like building things, taking them apart to see why they break, and getting outside to move around when I've been at the screen too long. Here's what I spend my free time on.

Building Security Tools

My favorite way to relax is building little security tools and scripts. Things like header scanners, endpoint and secret extractors, and scan dashboards started as weekend curiosity projects, not assignments. I enjoy the loop of having an idea, hacking it together, and seeing it actually work.

Breaking & Reverse Engineering

I like poking at how things work under the hood: reverse engineering, testing edge cases, and figuring out why something behaves the way it does. Generating reverse shells, reading source, and chasing protocol-level details is the kind of puzzle I lose hours to.

Automation Tinkering

If a task is boring and repetitive, I'd rather spend an afternoon writing a script to do it than do it by hand. Automating recon, reporting, and other manual workflows is half useful, half just satisfying to watch run.

AI & Security Experiments

Lately I've been experimenting with wiring AI into security tooling, like AI-assisted scan template suggestions and verification helpers. It's a fun space to mess around in because the tools keep changing and there's always something new to try.

Swimming

Away from the keyboard, swimming is how I reset. It's the one activity where I'm not staring at a screen, and a few laps clears my head better than anything else.

Cycling

I also enjoy getting out on my bicycle. It's part exercise, part exploring, a good excuse to be outside and cover some distance instead of sitting still all day.
